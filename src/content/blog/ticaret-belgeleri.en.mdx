While the vessel is at sea, someone asks "are the documents complete". If the answer takes days, the problem is not the documents but the list: which papers are required, which version counts, and who approved them were never written down in one place.

## The eighteen on the list

- **Carriage documents.** Bill of lading, air waybill, CMR: the papers that say whose hands the goods are in.
- **Origin and quality.** Certificate of origin, SGS or Bureau Veritas quality certificate, weight certificate, fumigation certificate.
- **Insurance and customs.** Marine cargo policy, export and import customs entries.
- **The money side.** SWIFT transfer proof, letter-of-credit copy and its amendments: whatever the payment hangs on.
- **Proforma and packing.** Commercial invoice, proforma invoice, packing list: the number's paper counterpart.

## From upload to approval

The work starts, not ends, at upload: the file passes mime, virus and checksum checks, then goes to a manager for approval. A rejection returns with its reason; the corrected file uploads again. Approved documents replicate to the account's Google Drive folder. A missing document or late payment holds the trade, with the reason visible to everyone.
